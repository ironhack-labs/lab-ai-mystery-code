## 🗂️ Case 1: Infinite Loop

🧠 Prompt used: "Why is this loop never ending?"

🤖 AI helped me identify that the loop was missing `i++`, causing an infinite loop.

💡 What I learned: Loops must always update their condition variable or they will run forever.

✔ Fix: Added `i++` and improved readability with comments.
